# AI-assistance disclosure

Jaden Chun is the sole human contributor to the original PAIEC project code. He directed the research questions, set the constraints and frozen methodological choices, approved the final model-selection decision, and is responsible for the published claims and release decisions.

OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex assisted with research organization, code drafting and development, environment troubleshooting, test orchestration, statistical verification scripts, analysis and report preparation, documentation, and editorial writing. These contributions are described at the project level; the records do not establish an exhaustive per-line contribution history or a complete assistant model/version history. This disclosure does not claim independent human re-execution of AI-assisted calculations.

The deployed frozen empirical Bayes and Beta predictors do not call language models, pretrained encoders or external APIs. AI assistance during development is distinct from the arithmetic performed by the submitted predictors. Inherited Stanford baseline code remains separately attributed and is not presented as independently authored project code.

This disclosure accompanies Manuscript V4.3 and the released code. It does not modify the manuscript or scientific results. Jaden Chun has approved MIT licensing for his original project code; third-party code and data retain their separate attribution and permissions.
